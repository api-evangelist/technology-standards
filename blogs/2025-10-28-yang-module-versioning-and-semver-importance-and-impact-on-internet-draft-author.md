YANG enables comprehensive network automation by providing a standardized way to model the configuration and state data of network elements. The NETMOD Working Group (WG) is getting ready to publish the long-awaited updated versioning scheme for YANG modules published by IETF. These changes will impact current authors of Internet-Drafts (I-Ds) that contain a YANG module.
